**This week's ask.** I need the risk picture. Not a list of everything that could
theoretically go wrong at a healthcare company — I can get that from a magazine. I need
*our* risks: what could actually hurt this company, how likely it is, how bad it would
be, and who owns doing something about it. Rank them. I will fund the top of that list
and I will tell you no on the bottom of it, and both of us need to be able to explain
why.

**Watch your footing.** The scariest thing on your list is probably not the most likely
thing, and the most likely thing is probably boring. Do not let the frightening item
outrank the probable one just because it's easier to picture. If you tell me something
is likely, I am going to ask how you know — and "it's in the news" is not an answer.

about this, and I've read all of it, twice. Two things to pass along. Ruth Kaplan's

The other thing: Dana read something over the weekend about a clinic group that got
hit with ransomware, and in Thursday's leadership meeting she asked me, straight out,
"are we a target?" I told her I'd get her a real answer. That's what I'm asking you for.

**This week's ask.** Tell me who would actually come at a company like this one, why,
and what they would do once they were in. Not a list of everybody who exists — the
two or three that fit *us*, with your reasoning. And for each step they'd take, I want
two columns: would it work here, and would we know.

**Watch your footing.** Threat-model the company you have, not the company you'd find
more interesting to defend. If your answer is the same answer a defense contractor
would write, one of us has made a mistake.

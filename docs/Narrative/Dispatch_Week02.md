# Dispatch — Week 2 · *The Job Description*

**From:** Dana Okafor, Chief Executive Officer
**To:** Security Program Lead
**Date:** Monday, January 25, 2027
**Re:** What exactly did we hire you to do?

---

**Where we stand.** Bhargavi tells me you spent your first week reading. Good — I'd
rather you read than spend. But here's my problem. I approved this hire, and if my
board asks me next month what that bought us, today I'd have to answer "a person."
That isn't a criticism of you. It's the gap I need you to close, and it's week two,
so now is exactly the right time to close it.

**This week's ask.** Tell me what we're buying. One page. What are you responsible
for, and — just as important — what are you *not*? What authority do you actually
have when you find something bad on a Friday afternoon? Who do you come to when
somebody tells you no? Call it a charter, call it a job description, I don't much
care what it's titled. I need something I can hold up in a leadership meeting, and
something St. Ansgar's risk office will accept as evidence that we have a program and
not a hobby.

**Watch your footing.** You will be tempted to ask me for tools. Don't — not yet.
Programs get built on paper before they get built with money, and a purchase I can't
tie back to a decision is the first line item I cut in the spring. Show me the shape
of the thing first. If the shape is right, I'll fund it.

**This feeds:** §3 — Governance & Program Charter.

— Dana
